  Trigger: When a complete V-model milestone is ready for final closure.

## When to Use

- Only when a V-model milestone is ready for final closure.

## Critical Patterns

- Shall strictly follow the provided resources and recursively review all referenced documents required to understand the current task.
- Shall verify the following points only for work products with upstream traceability to specifications assigned to the current or previous milestones:
  - Verify that the work products have complete vertical traceability from specifications to domain-specific implementations and horizontal traceability to their corresponding verification work products.
  - Verify that required work-product approval states have already been transitioned by the relevant review activities.
- Shall clearly state whether the scoped milestone is ready for closure, and identify any remaining gaps if it is not.
