# Hardware domain overview

The hardware domain does not define design-specific hardware development processes or traceability to hardware work products.

Hardware may be custom-developed or externally provided. In either case, the only requirement is the hardware-software interface required by the software domain.

## Glossary

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| Hardware-software interface | Work product defining the traceable elements from the hardware domain that are relevant for the software domain. |

## Processes

- [hw-sw-interface-definition](processes/hw-sw-interface-definition.md)

## Work products organization

The hardware domain shall be organized by process. Where applicable, processes shall have dedicated folders containing their generated work products. The structure shall be as follows:

```text
hw/
  cross_domain_interfaces/
```

The `hw/cross_domain_interfaces/` folder contains the work products generated by the `hw-sw-interface-definition` process (only Markdown documents: `.md`).
